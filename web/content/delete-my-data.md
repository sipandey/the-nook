# Deleting your information

You don’t need to ask us to delete your data — you can do it yourself, right now, from inside the app. This page explains exactly what that does, and what to do if you can’t get to it yourself.

> **If you can sign in**
>
> 1. Open Settings from inside the app
> 2. Scroll to the bottom and tap “Delete Account”
> 3. Type `DELETE` to confirm
>
> This is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period, no “restore within 30 days,” and no way for us to reverse it once it completes. It removes, in order: every journal entry, every manifestation and its detected signals, your notification preferences, and your encryption key material — then your account with our authentication provider itself. Once your account is gone, there’s nothing left on our servers tied to you except what’s described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) as necessary for legal or operational reasons unrelated to your journal content (we don’t currently retain anything in that category, but we’re stating the general rule honestly rather than promising an absolute we can’t verify forever).

## If you can’t sign in

Locked out, lost your passphrase and recovery phrase, or the account belongs to someone who’s no longer able to access it themselves — email us and we’ll delete the account manually. ## Want to keep your data, just take it with you?

That’s a different action — Settings has a separate “Export Data” option that decrypts your journal on your own device and downloads it, without deleting anything. Worth doing before you delete, if you want a copy.
